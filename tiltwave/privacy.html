Tiltwave — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Tiltwave is a casual tilt-to-roll marble game. There is no account to create. This policy explains what the app stores and what is shared with Apple and with our ad provider, Google.

Stored on your device

  • Your best distances (today's track and all time), your chosen marble, and how many runs you have finished. These never leave your phone.
  • Tilt readings from the motion sensor are used only to steer the marble while you play. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your distances are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and shown with your Game Center nickname. Apple handles this under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple ID.

Purchases (Apple)

Remove Ads and the Marble Pack are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can watch to continue a run, and a full-screen ad every few runs. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks), IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and performance data. Google uses this under the Google partner sites policy and Google's privacy policy.

You can decline tracking when the app asks, change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and, where required by law (for example in the EU and UK), manage your ad consent from the app's More > Privacy Choices. Buying Remove Ads stops the between-run ads.

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. We do not run our own analytics or servers, and we do not sell your data.

Children

Tiltwave is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. Game Center scores can be managed in your Game Center settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
