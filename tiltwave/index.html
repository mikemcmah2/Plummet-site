Tiltwave — Support

Tilt your iPhone to roll the marble down an endless neon track. No rails: roll off the edge and the run ends. Reach checkpoints to add time to the clock.

How to play

  • Hold the phone comfortably and tap Play. However you're holding it when the countdown starts counts as level.
  • Tip the top of the phone away from you to roll forward, tilt it back toward you to brake, and tilt left or right to steer. Hold a tilt and you keep speeding up.
  • Every 250 m there is a checkpoint that adds time. The run ends if you fall off or the clock reaches zero.
  • Once per run you can Continue: if you fell off, you go back on the track where you went over, with 10 extra seconds.

Daily Track

Everyone gets the same track each day. A new one starts at 00:00 UTC. Your best distance on it goes to the Daily Track leaderboard in Game Center.

Purchases

Remove Ads removes the ads between runs and makes Continue free. The Marble Pack adds the Gold, Glass, Lava and Aviator marbles. To get them back on a new device, open More > Restore Purchases.

Common questions

The marble drifts when I'm not tilting. Start a new run while holding the phone the way you want to play; that sets level.

My scores aren't on the leaderboard. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Daily scores only count on the day's track.
