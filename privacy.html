Plummet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Plummet is a casual game. We collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the app stores and shares.

What we collect

  • Player name — the name you type in the app. It is shown on the public leaderboard to other players. Please do not use your real full name or any personal information.
  • Gameplay data — your best distance, the number of skins you have unlocked, and the time of your best run.
  • Random device identifier — a random ID generated by the app the first time you open it. It is not your Apple advertising ID, and it is not linked to your real identity. It lets the leaderboard keep one row per player.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

Only to run the shared leaderboard and to save your progress. Your data is stored on your device and, for the leaderboard, on our database provider (Supabase).

Sharing

Your leaderboard name, best distance and skin count are visible to other players. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Inappropriate names

Names are filtered, and players can report a name from the leaderboard. We remove names that violate our rules. Report problems at the contact address below.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To have your leaderboard entry deleted from our servers, email us your player name and we will remove it.

Children

Plummet is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
