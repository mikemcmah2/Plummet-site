Plummet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Plummet is a casual game. We collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the app stores and shares.

What we collect

  • Player name — the name you type in the app. It is shown on the public leaderboard to other players. Please do not use your real full name or any personal information.
  • Gameplay data — your best distance, the number of skins you have unlocked, and the time of your best run.
  • Random device identifier — a random ID generated by the app the first time you open it. It is not your Apple advertising ID, and it is not linked to your real identity. It lets the leaderboard keep one row per player.
  • Usage and diagnostics data — anonymous events such as sessions started, run length, features used, and crashes or errors. It is tied only to the random device identifier above, never to your name or contact details.
  • Purchase information — if you buy something in the app (for example removing ads or unlocking a skin), Apple processes the payment. We receive only confirmation of what you own, through our purchase provider RevenueCat, so we can restore your purchases. We never see your payment details.
  • Advertising data — the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. If you are asked for permission to track, you can decline, and ads are then not personalized using that identifier.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not sell your data. We only read your advertising identifier if you grant tracking permission through Apple's prompt.

How we use it

To run the shared leaderboard, save your progress, understand how the game is used so we can improve it, restore purchases, and show ads that support the free game. Your data is stored on your device and, for the leaderboard and anonymous usage data, on our database provider (Supabase).

Sharing and third parties

Your leaderboard name, best distance and skin count are visible to other players. We share data with service providers that help run the app: Supabase (database), Google AdMob (ads) and RevenueCat (purchases). Each processes data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data.

Your choices

You can decline tracking in the iOS prompt or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can remove ads with an in-app purchase. You can ask us to delete your data using the contact address below.

Inappropriate names

Names are filtered, and players can report a name from the leaderboard. We remove names that violate our rules. Report problems at the contact address below.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To have your leaderboard entry and usage data deleted from our servers, email us your player name and we will remove them.

Children

Plummet is not directed at children under 13, and ads are configured for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
