Plummet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Plummet is a casual game. We collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the app stores and shares.

What we collect

  • Player name — a name the app generates for you (for example “BraveFalcon27”), which you can change to one you choose. It is shown on the public leaderboard to other players. Please do not use your real full name or any personal information.
  • Gameplay data — your best distance, the number of skins you have unlocked, and the time of your best run.
  • Random device identifier — a random ID generated by the app the first time you open it. It is not your Apple advertising ID, and it is not linked to your real identity. It lets the leaderboard keep one row per player.
  • Purchase information — if you buy something in the app (for example removing ads or unlocking a skin), Apple processes the payment. The app only checks with Apple which items you own, so it can restore your purchases. We never see your payment details.
  • Advertising data — the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and ad-interaction and diagnostic data to serve, measure and secure ads. If you are asked for permission to track, you can decline, and ads are then not personalized using that identifier. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not sell your data. We only read your advertising identifier if you grant tracking permission through Apple's prompt.

How we use it

To run the shared leaderboard, save your progress, restore purchases, and show ads that support the free game. Your data is stored on your device and, for the leaderboard, on our database provider (Supabase).

Sharing and third parties

Your leaderboard name, best distance and skin count are visible to other players. We share data with service providers that help run the app: Supabase (database) and Google AdMob (ads). Purchases are handled by Apple. Each processes data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data.

Consent for users in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form (provided by Google) before any ads are loaded. You can accept or decline, and you can change your choice at any time from the “Privacy options” link at the bottom of the in-app Store. If you decline, ads are not personalized, or are not shown, as the law requires. You can also remove ads with an in-app purchase.

Your choices

You can decline tracking in the iOS prompt or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can remove ads with an in-app purchase. You can ask us to delete your data using the contact address below.

Inappropriate names

Names are filtered, and players can report a name from the leaderboard. We remove names that violate our rules. Report problems at the contact address below.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To have your leaderboard entry deleted from our servers, email us your player name and we will remove it.

Children

Plummet is not directed at children under 13, and ads are configured for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
